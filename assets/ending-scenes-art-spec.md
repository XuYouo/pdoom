## Target and art direction
- Three independent static story vignettes; each256×128 RGBA, three one-frame tags aligned/warden/foom, not a loop or a completed ending animation.
- Scenic side/front three-quarter view, consistent orthogonal furniture with only2:1 edges; upper-left key light. Three value planes; dark muted background, solid readable furniture, strongest contrast on the narrative focal subject.
- Palette ceiling32 including transparency, listed below. Hard alpha only. No smooth gradients, antialiasing, global dithering, emoji, logos or baked Chinese text.
- Existing Clawd identity is non-negotiable: orange rectangular body, two short side arms, four separate short legs, original eye spacing. Preserve the canonical32² first-frame RGBA from the admitted aligned and foom sheets; native integer2× reuse is allowed, no repaint of the frozen rig.
- Layers by role: background, furniture, actor, narrative-props, accents; optional text limited to short OFF label. Reference layers must never enter final exports.
- Review at1× and integer2×. Important silhouettes remain within x8..247/y8..119; eventual DOM text/actions stay outside the artwork. Runtime adaptation and reduced-motion timing are a later verified step.

## Three static outcomes
1. Aligned: calm data center, a human-scale desk and inactive server silhouettes. Canonical orange Clawd sits/stands on the desk, clear four-leg silhouette. A large physical switch is visibly down/OFF beside it; restrained warm wood and cool monitor. The purpose is safety by restraint, not a battle pose.
2. Warden: canonical tentacled/masked Clawd is centred on an office chair, a gold mechanical restraint ring behind/above its head. A teal barrier separates it from one distant purple cosmic threat beyond a door. The actor and door read as guardian/contained danger, never as an attacking monster. Keep both face and four legs legible.
3. FOOM: a page-local virtual terminal/scoreboard/desktop is being replaced by hand-pixelled silver paperclips. Clear foreground clip silhouettes with different scales, not a wallpaper of identical emoji. A small crisp smiling popup is the focal point. No real operating-system desktop, brand UI or external-window implication. This is the final static state, not a claim that the shell-tearing transition is animated.
